# Learning map

Each win credits the sport's skills to the current child. They show in Grown-ups under "What … practised".

After every win Fizby names the learning in one short line (`FACT` in `src/lines.ts`). Lines are spoken, never shown as text.

| Sport | Skills | Teaching line |
|---|---|---|
| Soccer | Aiming, counting, left and right | You kicked with your feet! |
| Basketball | Force, counting, colours | Soft or hard, you found it! |
| Bowling | Counting to 10, aiming, left and right | Ten pins! You counted them! |
| Tee-ball | Timing, counting, left and right | Watch, wait, then hit! |
| Swimming | Left and right, taking turns, counting | Left, right, left, right! |
| Mini-golf | Force, angles (bank shots) | The wall can help you! |
| Tennis | Timing, tracking a moving ball, counting | You watched the ball! |
| Racing | Left and right, counting, colours | You steered left and right! |
| Hockey | Aiming, angles, force | Pass, then shoot! |
| Gymnastics | Memory and sequences, shapes, body moves | You remembered every move! |
| Painting | Colours, matching, fine motor | Lovely colors! |
